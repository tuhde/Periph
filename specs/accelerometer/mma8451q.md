# Chip Spec: MMA8451Q

**Manufacturer:** NXP Semiconductors (formerly Freescale)  
**Datasheet:** `datasheets/accelerometer/mma845x.pdf`  
**Category:** accelerometer  
**Transports:** I²C

## Overview

The MMA8451Q is a low-power, three-axis capacitive MEMS accelerometer with 14-bit output (4096 counts/*g* at ±2 *g*), dynamically selectable ±2 / ±4 / ±8 *g* full scale and output data rates from 1.56 Hz to 800 Hz. It offloads the host with a 32-sample FIFO (fill / circular / trigger), a high-pass-filtered output option, and five embedded detectors: freefall / motion, transient (high-pass filtered motion), single / double / directional tap ("pulse"), portrait / landscape + back / front orientation with programmable hysteresis, and Auto-SLEEP / Auto-WAKE rate switching. Seven interrupt sources are routable to two pins (INT1, INT2). Four oversampling modes trade noise against current (6 µA – 165 µA), and per-axis user offset registers (2 mg/LSB) allow post-assembly zero-*g* calibration. Typical uses: orientation and tilt, tap / shake gestures, motion wake-up for battery devices, freefall and shock logging.

The datasheet committed here (Rev 8, 02/2013) documents the **MMA8451Q** only. The MMA8452Q (12-bit) and MMA8453Q (10-bit) share the register map (datasheet Table 8 lists the family's resolution and sensitivity: 14 / 12 / 10 bits, 4096 / 1024 / 256 counts/*g*) but differ in resolution and in `WHO_AM_I`; they are **not** covered by this spec — each would need its own entry.

## Transport Configuration

### I²C
- **Address:** `0x1C` (SA0 = GND) — `0x1D` (SA0 = VDDIO); write bytes `0x38` / `0x3A`, read bytes `0x39` / `0x3B`
- **Max clock:** 400 kHz in the AC table; the datasheet front page claims operation to 2.25 MHz with a 4.7 kΩ pull-up (not characterised — the driver assumes ≤ 400 kHz)
- **Identity register:** `0x0D` = `0x1A` (`WHO_AM_I`) — read with mask `0xFF`, expected `0x1A`
- **Probe safety:** `register_pointer` — the identity read has no side effects

I²C framing is the plain register-pointer protocol: write the register address, repeated START, read. **There is no auto-increment bit**: a multi-byte read always auto-increments the pointer, but only within one transaction (the internal pointer is cleared at STOP). The datasheet requires **repeated START** for reads. Multi-byte writes also auto-increment. Reserved registers (everything not listed in the Register Map) must not be written.

## Pin Configuration

| Pin | Active | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| INT1 | low (default), push-pull | interrupt output; polarity `IPOL` and drive `PP_OD` in `CTRL_REG3` |
| INT2 | low (default), push-pull | interrupt output; same settings (shared with INT1) |
| SA0 | — | GND → `0x1C`, VDDIO → `0x1D`; must not float |
| BYP | — | bypass capacitor, 75 nF – 470 nF (100 nF recommended) |
| VDD / VDDIO | — | VDD 1.95–3.6 V; VDDIO 1.6–3.6 V (no sequencing required; VDDIO may exceed VDD) |
| NC | — | leave unconnected |

Both interrupt pins use the same polarity / drive configuration. Open-drain (`PP_OD` = 1) allows wired-OR of several interrupt lines.

### Bit Fields

#### `STATUS` (`0x00`, `F_MODE` = 0)

| Bit 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ZYXOW | ZOW | YOW | XOW | ZYXDR | ZDR | YDR | XDR |

`*OW` = a new sample overwrote an unread one; `*DR` = new sample available. `ZYXDR` clears once all three MSB registers have been read; `xDR` clears on reading the axis' MSB register.

#### `F_STATUS` (`0x00`, `F_MODE` > 0) and `F_SETUP` (`0x09`)

| Bits | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| `0x00` 7 | F_OVF | FIFO overflow (32 samples and another arrived) |
| `0x00` 6 | F_WMRK_FLAG | FIFO count ≥ watermark; in trigger mode also "trigger event detected" |
| `0x00` 5:0 | F_CNT<5:0> | Samples in the FIFO, 0–32 |
| `0x09` 7:6 | F_MODE<1:0> | `00` disabled, `01` circular (oldest discarded), `10` fill (stops accepting when full), `11` trigger |
| `0x09` 5:0 | F_WMRK<5:0> | Watermark (0 disables the flag); in trigger mode the number of pre-trigger samples |

Reading `F_STATUS` clears `SRC_FIFO` in `INT_SOURCE`. The FIFO is flushed when it is disabled, on an automatic ODR change (Auto-WAKE/SLEEP) unless `FIFO_GATE` is set, and on STANDBY → ACTIVE. The mode must go through `00` to switch between enabled modes. Reading a byte at `0x01` returns and removes the oldest sample; the FIFO is meant to be drained with multi-byte reads.

#### `TRIG_CFG` (`0x0A`)

| Bit 5 | Bit 4 | Bit 3 | Bit 2 |
|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| Trig_TRANS | Trig_LNDPRT | Trig_PULSE | Trig_FF_MT |

#### `SYSMOD` (`0x0B`)

| Bits | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| 7 | FGERR | FIFO gate error (cleared by emptying the FIFO) |
| 6:2 | FGT<4:0> | ODR periods since `FGERR` |
| 1:0 | SYSMOD | `00` STANDBY, `01` WAKE, `10` SLEEP |

#### `INT_SOURCE` (`0x0C`)

| Bit 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SRC_ASLP | SRC_FIFO | SRC_TRANS | SRC_LNDPRT | SRC_PULSE | SRC_FF_MT | — | SRC_DRDY |

Cleared by reading: `SYSMOD` (ASLP), `F_STATUS` (FIFO), `TRANSIENT_SRC`, `PL_STATUS`, `PULSE_SRC`, `FF_MT_SRC`, the X / Y / Z data (DRDY). **Reading `STATUS` (`0x00`) does not clear `SRC_DRDY`.**

#### `PL_STATUS` (`0x10`), `PL_CFG` (`0x11`), `PL_BF_ZCOMP` (`0x13`), `P_L_THS_REG` (`0x14`)

| Reg | Bit 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|-----|-------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PL_STATUS` | NEWLP | LO | — | — | — | LAPO1 | LAPO0 | BAFRO |
| `PL_CFG` | DBCNTM | PL_EN | — | — | — | — | — | — |
| `PL_BF_ZCOMP` | BKFR1 | BKFR0 | — | — | — | ZLOCK2 | ZLOCK1 | ZLOCK0 |
| `P_L_THS_REG` | P_L_THS4 | P_L_THS3 | P_L_THS2 | P_L_THS1 | P_L_THS0 | HYS2 | HYS1 | HYS0 |

`LAPO` = `00` portrait up, `01` portrait down, `10` landscape right, `11` landscape left. `BAFRO` = 0 front, 1 back. `LO` = Z-tilt lockout (orientation frozen). `NEWLP` is set on the first detection after STANDBY → ACTIVE and on any change; cleared by reading `PL_STATUS`. Orientation is locked while any axis exceeds 1.25 *g*. `DBCNTM` = 1 clears the debounce counter when the condition is lost; 0 decrements it.

| Z-lock angle | 14° | 18° | 21° | 25° | 29° (default) | 33° | 37° | 42° |

| `BKFR` | Back → front | Front → back |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| `00` | Z < 80° or Z > 280° | Z > 100° and Z < 260° |

| `P_L_THS<4:0>` | `0x07` | `0x09` | `0x0C` | `0x0D` | `0x0F` | `0x10` | `0x13` | `0x14` | `0x17` | `0x19` |

`HYS` adds 0 / ±4 / ±7 / ±11 / ±14 / ±17 / ±21 / ±24° for `HYS` = 0…7 (default `100` = ±14°). The condition `THS + HYS > 0` and `THS + HYS < 32` (register values, decimal) must hold for the detector to work.

#### `FF_MT_CFG` (`0x15`), `FF_MT_SRC` (`0x16`), `FF_MT_THS` (`0x17`)

| Reg | Bit 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|-----|-------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `FF_MT_CFG` | ELE | OAE | ZEFE | YEFE | XEFE | — | — | — |
| `FF_MT_SRC` | EA | — | ZHE | ZHP | YHE | YHP | XHE | XHP |
| `FF_MT_THS` | DBCNTM | THS6 | THS5 | THS4 | THS3 | THS2 | THS1 | THS0 |

`OAE` = 0 → **freefall** (all enabled axes ≤ threshold, AND); `OAE` = 1 → **motion** (any enabled axis > threshold, OR). `ELE` = 1 latches the flags in `FF_MT_SRC` until it is read; with `ELE` = 0 the flags reflect real time and **reading `FF_MT_SRC` does not clear them**. `xHP` = 1 means negative *g*. Per-axis flags read 0 if the axis' `xEFE` is 0. In freefall mode with `ELE` = 0 and `DBCNTM` = 0, `EA` can clear only after the debounce delay.

#### `TRANSIENT_CFG` (`0x1D`), `TRANSIENT_SRC` (`0x1E`), `TRANSIENT_THS` (`0x1F`)

| Reg | Bit 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|-----|-------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `TRANSIENT_CFG` | — | — | — | ELE | ZTEFE | YTEFE | XTEFE | HPF_BYP |
| `TRANSIENT_SRC` | — | EA | ZTRANSE | Z_Trans_Pol | YTRANSE | Y_Trans_Pol | XTRANSE | X_Trans_Pol |
| `TRANSIENT_THS` | DBCNTM | THS6…THS0 | | | | | | |

`HPF_BYP` = 1 makes the function behave like motion detection. Reading `TRANSIENT_SRC` clears the flags and the interrupt. Polarity 1 = negative *g*.

#### `PULSE_CFG` (`0x21`), `PULSE_SRC` (`0x22`)

| Reg | Bit 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|-----|-------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PULSE_CFG` | DPA | ELE | ZDPEFE | ZSPEFE | YDPEFE | YSPEFE | XDPEFE | XSPEFE |
| `PULSE_SRC` | EA | AxZ | AxY | AxX | DPE | PolZ | PolY | PolX |

`DPA` (double-pulse abort) suspends double-tap detection if a pulse starts during the latency window and ends before it expires. `DPE` = 1 → double pulse, 0 → single pulse. Pol = 1 → negative. Reading `PULSE_SRC` clears all flags and the interrupt (with `ELE` = 1 the status is frozen until then). The latency timer (`PULSE_LTCY`) must be set for single as well as double pulses.

#### `CTRL_REG1` (`0x2A`)

| Bits | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| 7:6 | ASLP_RATE<1:0> | Sleep-mode ODR: `00` 50 Hz, `01` 12.5 Hz, `10` 6.25 Hz, `11` 1.56 Hz |
| 5:3 | DR<2:0> | ODR: `000` 800 Hz, `001` 400 Hz, `010` 200 Hz, `011` 100 Hz, `100` 50 Hz, `101` 12.5 Hz, `110` 6.25 Hz, `111` 1.56 Hz |
| 2 | LNOISE | 1 = reduced-noise mode; **range limited to ±4 *g*** |
| 1 | F_READ | 1 = fast read: 8-bit data, auto-increment skips the LSB registers (applies to the FIFO too; change only when `F_MODE` = 0) |
| 0 | ACTIVE | 0 = STANDBY, 1 = ACTIVE |

Only `ACTIVE` may be written in ACTIVE mode; every other field needs STANDBY.

#### `CTRL_REG2` (`0x2B`)

| Bits | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| 7 | ST | 1 = self-test (electrostatic force applied) |
| 6 | RST | 1 = software reset: all registers reset, I²C interface reset, bit self-clears |
| 4:3 | SMODS<1:0> | Oversampling mode in SLEEP |
| 2 | SLPE | 1 = Auto-SLEEP enabled |
| 1:0 | MODS<1:0> | Oversampling mode in WAKE / ACTIVE: `00` normal, `01` low-noise low-power, `10` high resolution, `11` low power |

`RST` is writable in STANDBY and ACTIVE; reading it returns 0.

#### `CTRL_REG3` (`0x2C`)

| Bits | Name | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| 7 | FIFO_GATE | 1 = block new FIFO input around WAKE/SLEEP transitions until the FIFO is emptied |
| 6 | WAKE_TRANS | Transient can wake from SLEEP |

| Bit 7 | 6 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|-------|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ASLP | FIFO | TRANS | LNDPRT | PULSE | FF_MT | — | DRDY |

## Initialization Sequence

1. Power up; I²C is ready ≈ 350 µs (500 µs max, with the recommended 100 nF BYP capacitor) after VDDIO is on and VDD > VDDmin. Wait ≥ 1 ms.
2. Read `WHO_AM_I` (`0x0D`); verify it equals `0x1A`.
3. Software reset: write `CTRL_REG2` ← `0x40` (`RST`). The I²C interface is reset by it, so the write may complete without ACK on the data phase; wait ≥ 1 ms (boot ≤ 0.5 ms) and re-read until `CTRL_REG2` returns 0 (retry on NACK, up to 10 ms).
4. (Device is now in STANDBY with all registers at reset values.) Write `XYZ_DATA_CFG` ← `0x00` (±2 *g*, no high-pass output).
5. Write `CTRL_REG2` ← `0x00` (normal oversampling in WAKE and SLEEP, Auto-SLEEP off).
6. Write `CTRL_REG1` ← `0x19` (`DR` = `011` 100 Hz, `LNOISE` = 0, `F_READ` = 0, `ACTIVE` = 1).
7. Wait ≥ 2/ODR + 1 ms (21 ms at 100 Hz) for the STANDBY → ACTIVE turn-on; then the first sample is valid.

## Interrupt

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| INT pins | `INT1` and `INT2`; polarity `IPOL` (default active-low) and drive `PP_OD` (default push-pull), shared by both pins |
| Level | 3 (multiple INT lines; every source individually routable) |
| Condition(s) | new data; FIFO watermark / overflow; freefall / motion; transient; tap (single / double); orientation change; Auto-SLEEP / WAKE transition |
| Clear mechanism | per source: read the corresponding source register (see `INT_SOURCE`); data-ready: read the X, Y, Z data |

### Interrupt sources

The source constants equal the `INT_SOURCE` / `CTRL_REG4` / `CTRL_REG5` bit positions:

| Constant | Value | Condition | Cleared by reading |
|----------|-------|-----------|--------------------|
| `SOURCE_DATA_READY` | `0x01` | New XYZ sample (`ZYXDR` / `ZYXOW`) | the X, Y, Z data |
| `SOURCE_FF_MT` | `0x04` | Freefall / motion (`FF_MT_SRC.EA`) | `FF_MT_SRC` |
| `SOURCE_PULSE` | `0x08` | Single / double tap (`PULSE_SRC.EA`) | `PULSE_SRC` |
| `SOURCE_ORIENTATION` | `0x10` | Orientation change (`PL_STATUS.NEWLP`) | `PL_STATUS` |
| `SOURCE_TRANSIENT` | `0x20` | Transient (`TRANSIENT_SRC.EA`) | `TRANSIENT_SRC` |
| `SOURCE_FIFO` | `0x40` | FIFO overflow or watermark | `F_STATUS` (`0x00`) |
| `SOURCE_AUTO_SLEEP` | `0x80` | WAKE ↔ SLEEP transition | `SYSMOD` |

### Full driver interrupt API

| Method | Signature | Description |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| `on_interrupt` | `on_interrupt(callback, pin=1)` | Subscribe on INT`pin` (1 or 2); callback receives the status int |
| `off_interrupt` | `off_interrupt(pin=1)` | Unsubscribe |
| `poll_interrupt` | `poll_interrupt() -> int` | Read `INT_SOURCE` and return it; for every set bit except `SOURCE_DATA_READY` the driver also reads the matching source register so the flag (and the pin) clears. `SOURCE_DATA_READY` is left set — clear it by reading the data |
| `enable_interrupt` | `enable_interrupt(source, pin=1)` | Set `INT_EN_*` in `CTRL_REG4` and route to INT`pin` in `CTRL_REG5` (STANDBY handling is internal) |
| `disable_interrupt` | `disable_interrupt(source)` | Clear `INT_EN_*` |

`enable_interrupt` only routes the source; the detector behind it is configured with the `set_*` methods in the Full class.

### Status register bit layout (`poll_interrupt()` return value)

Same as `INT_SOURCE` (bit 7 `SOURCE_AUTO_SLEEP` … bit 0 `SOURCE_DATA_READY`; bit 1 unused).

## Implementation Stages

### Minimal

Goal: read X, Y, Z acceleration in *g* with sensible defaults; no configuration required beyond the connection.

| Operation | Parameters | Returns | Notes |
|-----------|------------|---------|-------|
| `init` | `connection` | — | Runs the Initialization Sequence (±2 *g*, 100 Hz, 14-bit, normal oversampling) |
| `read` | — | `(x, y, z)` floats | Acceleration in *g*; one 6-byte read of `0x01–0x06` |

**Sensible defaults:** ±2 *g* (4096 counts/*g*), 100 Hz ODR, 14-bit output, normal oversampling, low-noise off, high-pass output off, FIFO disabled, all detectors and interrupt routing off, interrupts active-low push-pull, offsets 0.

### Full

Goal: expose complete chip functionality. Extends Minimal. Methods that change a "STANDBY-only" register call `standby()`, write, and restore `ACTIVE` internally (and re-wait the turn-on time); the user need not manage the mode.

| Operation | Parameters | Returns | Notes |
|-----------|------------|---------|-------|
| *(inherits Minimal)* | | | |

## Data Conversion

Acceleration (14-bit, two's complement, left-justified in the 16-bit MSB:LSB pair; MSB register first):

```
raw16 = int16(OUT_x_MSB << 8 | OUT_x_LSB)      # bits 1:0 of LSB are always 0
counts = raw16 >> 2                             # 14-bit, arithmetic shift
acceleration_g = counts / counts_per_g          # 4096 (±2 g), 2048 (±4 g), 1024 (±8 g)
```

8-bit (fast read, `F_READ` = 1; MSB register only):

```
counts = int8(OUT_x_MSB)
acceleration_g = counts / counts_per_g          # 64 (±2 g, 15.6 mg), 32 (±4 g, 31.25 mg), 16 (±8 g, 62.5 mg)
```

Sensitivity is documented as 4096 / 2048 / 1024 counts/*g* (datasheet Table 2); the datasheet notes a ~3 % sensitivity shift when switching to low-power oversampling or from 800 Hz to another ODR. Output values are in *g* (no m/s²).

Thresholds (7-bit, 0.063 *g*/LSB, valid to 8 *g* regardless of the full-scale setting; 4 *g* max with `LNOISE`):

```
THS code = clamp(round(threshold_g / 0.063), 0, 127)      # FF_MT_THS, TRANSIENT_THS, PULSE_THSx
```

Offsets: `OFF_x = clamp(round(offset_g / 0.002), -128, 127)` as signed 8-bit (2 mg/LSB, ±0.256 *g*).

Debounce and time steps depend on the ODR and oversampling mode (datasheet Tables 27, 38, 43). **Debounce step** for `PL_COUNT`, `FF_MT_COUNT`, `TRANSIENT_COUNT`, in ms (maximum = 255 × step):

| ODR (Hz) | Normal | Low-noise low-power | High resolution | Low power |
|----------|--------|---------------------|-----------------|-----------|
| 800 | 1.25 | 1.25 | 1.25 | 1.25 |
| 400 | 2.5 | 2.5 | 2.5 | 2.5 |
| 200 | 5 | 5 | 2.5 | 5 |
| 100 | 10 | 10 | 2.5 | 10 |
| 50 | 20 | 20 | 2.5 | 20 |
| 12.5 | 20 | 80 | 2.5 | 80 |
| 6.25 | 20 | 80 | 2.5 | 160 |
| 1.56 | 20 | 80 | 2.5 | 160 |

`COUNT = clamp(round(debounce_ms / step_ms), 0, 255)`.

**Pulse time steps** in ms (`PULSE_TMLT` uses Tables 50 / 51; `PULSE_LTCY` and `PULSE_WIND` use Tables 53 / 54 — equal to 56 / 57 — maximum = 255 × step). With `Pulse_LPF_EN` = 1 (the `lpf` argument):

| 400 | 2.5 / 2.5 / 2.5 / 2.5 | 5 / 5 / 5 / 5 |
| 200 | 5 / 5 / 2.5 / 5 | 10 / 10 / 5 / 10 |

## Timing Constraints

- **I²C ready after power-up:** ≈ 350 µs typical, 500 µs max after VDDIO on and VDD > VDDmin (BYP = 100 nF); boot current ≤ 0.5 ms. The driver waits ≥ 1 ms.
- **Turn-on STANDBY → ACTIVE:** 2/ODR + 1 ms; power-down → ACTIVE 2/ODR + 2 ms (datasheet Table 3). The driver waits 2/ODR + 2 ms after setting `ACTIVE` (22 ms at 100 Hz, 1.28 s at 1.56 Hz).
- **First data after ACTIVE:** the first sample set is ready after the turn-on time. **Conformance-checked**: `first_data_ready` — sigrok annotation pair `active_start` (write to `CTRL_REG1` `0x2A` setting `ACTIVE` = 1) → `active_ready` (first read of `STATUS` `0x00` returning `ZYXDR` = 1). See `specs/accelerometer/mma8451q_timing.conf`.
- **Reset:** after `RST` wait ≥ 1 ms (boot ≤ 0.5 ms); the I²C interface is reset, so a NACK right after the write is expected.
- **ODR change / mode change:** the datasheet notes a ~3 % sensitivity shift when changing from 800 Hz or switching to low-power oversampling; discard samples for ≥ 2/ODR after a change.
- **Debounce counters:** a write to `F_WMRK`, `*_THS` or `*_COUNT` resets that detector's debounce counter.
- **FIFO and ODR:** the FIFO fills at the system ODR (the sleep ODR while in SLEEP); it is flushed on every automatic ODR change unless `FIFO_GATE` is set.

## Implementation Notes

- **Register-addressed:** accept `RegisterConnection` (1-byte register address) and call `connection.read(reg, length)` / `connection.write(reg, data)` directly — no chip-local `_read_reg`/`_write_reg` and no `bus_type` branch (see `specs/feature_register_access_design.md`). The chip has no auto-increment bit; `connection.read(0x01, 6)` auto-increments by itself, as required by the datasheet's repeated-START read.
- **STANDBY-only configuration:** most configuration registers (footnote 4 of datasheet Table 12) can only be written in STANDBY. Full wraps writes: read `CTRL_REG1`, clear `ACTIVE`, write, set `ACTIVE`, wait the turn-on time. `STATUS`-type registers are reset on STANDBY → ACTIVE; the FIFO is flushed.
- **Interrupt polarity default is active-low** (`IPOL` = 0), the opposite of LIS3DH's default. `init` leaves it as reset; wired MCU inputs should expect a falling edge.
- **Read-to-clear is per source** (see `INT_SOURCE`); reading `STATUS` does not clear `SRC_DRDY`. `FF_MT_SRC` with `ELE` = 0 is a live status and is **not** cleared by reading.
- **FIFO register behaviour:** with `F_MODE` > 0, `0x00` becomes `F_STATUS` and `new_data_available` is invalid; use `fifo_level`. The datasheet's auto-increment column for `F_MODE` > 0 / `F_READ` is hard to read; the driver reads one sample per transaction starting at `0x01` (6 bytes, or 3 in 8-bit mode) and never relies on the pointer behaviour across samples. The FIFO is intended to be emptied in multi-byte reads.
- **8-bit mode vs FIFO:** `F_READ` can be changed only when `F_MODE` = 0; it applies to both the output registers and the FIFO.
- **Low-noise mode:** limits the dynamic range to ±4 *g* and any threshold above 4 *g* is never reached; `set_low_noise` and `set_range` enforce the combination.
- **Threshold range:** thresholds are valid to 8 *g* even at ±2 / ±4 *g* full scale (the detector uses an 8 *g* internal scale); the driver does not clamp them to the selected range.
- **Orientation constraints:** `THS + HYS` (register values) must lie in 1…31; the trip angles are the discrete Table 32 values; orientation freezes above 1.25 *g* on any axis. Z-lock and back/front angles are accurate to ±2°.
- **Self-test:** `self_test()` — put the device in STANDBY, set ±4 *g*, 100 Hz, normal oversampling, ACTIVE; average a few samples; set `ST` = 1; wait ≥ 2/ODR; average again; clear `ST`; compute `d = after − before` in 14-bit counts. The datasheet lists only typical changes (X +181, Y +255, Z +1680 counts, "one direction only"; the test conditions in Table 2 are partly garbled, "FS 4g mode" is assumed) and no limits, so `ok` is `d > typical / 2` on every axis — a heuristic; verify on hardware.
- **Offset registers:** 2 mg/LSB, ±0.256 *g*; volatile (lost at reset and power-down), written only in STANDBY. See application note AN4069 for the calibration procedure; the driver provides the register access only.
- **Auto-SLEEP:** while in SLEEP the ODR and oversampling come from `ASLP_RATE` / `SMODS`; wake sources are limited to transient, orientation, pulse and freefall/motion; data-ready and FIFO do not wake the device, and `SRC_ASLP` does not restart the timer. `SYSMOD` reports the current state; reading it clears `SRC_ASLP`.
- **Siblings:** MMA8452Q (12-bit) and MMA8453Q (10-bit) have the same map with lower resolution (counts/*g* 1024 and 256 at ±2 *g*) and different `WHO_AM_I` values; no compatibility is assumed or claimed beyond the family table in the datasheet.
- **I²C address:** other addresses are available "at customer request" (factory programmed); the driver accepts any 7-bit address in its constructor's connection.
- **Datasheet revision:** the committed datasheet is Rev 8 (02/2013) (Adafruit-hosted copy of the Freescale document).

## Sigrok Decoder

Decoder id `mma8451q`, input `['i2c']`. Matches I²C addresses `0x1C` and `0x1D`. Annotates every register in the Register Map by name; decodes `STATUS` / `F_STATUS`, `SYSMOD`, `INT_SOURCE`, `XYZ_DATA_CFG`, `HP_FILTER_CUTOFF`, `PL_STATUS`, `PL_CFG`, `PL_BF_ZCOMP`, `P_L_THS_REG`, `FF_MT_CFG` / `SRC`, `TRANSIENT_CFG` / `SRC`, `PULSE_CFG` / `SRC`, `CTRL_REG1`–`CTRL_REG5`, `F_SETUP` and `TRIG_CFG` into named flags; decodes `DR`, `FS`, `MODS` and `F_MODE` into human-readable values; assembles the burst read of `0x01–0x06` into 14-bit signed values converted to *g* at the last-seen range (8-bit when `F_READ` is set). `WHO_AM_I` reads are checked against `0x1A`. For the First-data-ready Timing Constraint the decoder emits the `timing` annotation row markers `active_start` (write to `CTRL_REG1` `0x2A` setting `ACTIVE` = 1) and `active_ready` (first read of `STATUS` `0x00` returning `ZYXDR` = 1), shaped per `specs/sigrok_annotations.md`'s Variable-Length Annotation Convention.
